Go-to-market roadmap

A phased path to going global

Phase 1

2025 — Domestic Revival
  • Relaunch the central kitchen with an automated skewering & freezing line
  • Deploy 10 pilot kiosks across Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru
  • Launch the hot-pack take-away concept and online ordering (Grab, ShopeeFood)

Phase 2

2026–27 — National Scale
  • Expand to 50 locations nationwide across all three formats
  • Secure certified halal & food-safety accreditation for export
  • Partner with petrol stations and launch a corporate & events catering arm

Phase 3

2028 — International Entry
  • Open the first overseas kiosk in the Middle East (Arabic brand variant)
  • Target ASEAN: Singapore, Brunei, Indonesia, and Thailand
  • License localized models for Korea & Japan; frozen retail packs for GCC
